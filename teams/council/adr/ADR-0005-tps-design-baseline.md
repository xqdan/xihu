# ADR-0005: TPS/usr design baseline and its change control

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: accepted for repository modeling governance

## Context

The published Final Tuning point is 1101.77 TPS/usr (P1, MC640, TP32,
frequency fixed at 1.0 GHz, physical basis of decision 6). The software and
hardware choices behind it were spread across OPT comments, commit messages,
`00_CURRENT_STATE.md` and the hardware unit docs. Nothing listed the full set of
mechanisms the claim depends on, what each one contributes, or how thin the
margin is (78.95 µs of raw against the 854.70 µs budget).

## Decision

1. `docs/architecture/21_TPS_DESIGN_BASELINE.md` is the single design description of
   the published TPS/usr point. It covers the time ledger, the hardware per unit,
   the software mechanisms, ablations, sensitivities, evidence classes and open
   items.
2. Its numbers come from `teams/hardware/inputs/k3_mc_baseline.json#tpsDesign`.
   That block is generated by `integration/detailed/k3_tps_design_baseline.js`, which
   `integration/pipelines/sync_baseline_spec.js` calls. Every ablation is recomputed from the
   model.
3. `tests/regression/test_tps_design_baseline.js` enforces the following:
   - the spec block equals a fresh rebuild;
   - every listed mechanism is enabled, and switching it back alone lowers TPS
     or makes the point infeasible;
   - the no-effect switches really have no effect;
   - the key numbers in doc 21 match the spec.
4. The status is `BASELINE`, not `FROZEN`. Per ADR-0003, no configuration that
   rests on MC640 (B-002), unregistered τ physics (B-008), unvalidated FP8 KV
   precision (B-001) or uncalibrated PPA coefficients (B-006) may be frozen.
5. Change control:
   - Any change to OPT, GAIN, A.TECH, A.LIMITS, simulator scheduling semantics
     or the search space must re-run `npm run search:final`, then
     `npm run baseline:sync`, then `npm run model:planning` (its artifacts hash the
     spec), and update doc 21 in the same commit.
   - A new mechanism enters `MECHANISMS` only if it has an OPT switch, a
     service-line trace in the ledger, and a TPS loss when switched back alone.
     Otherwise it goes into `NO_EFFECT`.
   - The frequency is fixed at 1.0 GHz. Compute is sized by core and engine
     counts and shapes; buying compute by lowering the clock is a search-space
     change and needs its own decision.
   - The physical basis of the die (process area scaling, matrix density,
     cooling and its power limits) lives in `integration/detailed/k3_physical_basis.js`
     and is a change of the same class as A.TECH/A.LIMITS.
6. Physical basis, decided 2026-09-25 (all `ASSUMPTION`, B-006 / O-015):
   - compute-die area on Samsung SF4-class 4 nm, scaled from the N4-reference
     coefficients with public node figures: logic ×1.277 (CPP × MMP, SF4E
     57 × 32 nm vs N4 51 × 28 nm), SRAM ×1.248 (HD bitcell 0.0262 vs
     0.021 µm²), UCIe/RDMA PHY ×1;
   - matrix density 3.2 TF/mm² at 1 GHz on the N4 reference (was 1.6), with the
     SF4 logic factor on top;
   - liquid cooling (cold plate): die 300 W, card 2800 W (was 260 W / 2400 W).
   Power, frequency and bandwidth coefficients are unchanged.

## Consequences

- The shared-SRAM port scaling currently costs 16.15 mm² (SF4) and 5.69 W per die
  (45.51 W per card) for 0.06 TPS. That is now visible and can be reclaimed by a
  later decision.
- The published point needs the denser matrix and the liquid-cooled limits
  together: the power returns UCIe to 128 lanes (MC no longer capped by the
  UCIe port) and buys H compute; either alone adds under 10 TPS.
- The published point exceeds the 1050 architecture gate. The gate stays not-met:
  it requires the manufacturable MC route in the detailed tile model.
- Doc 21 will fail the test if its numbers drift from the model. Hand edits
  alone therefore cannot keep it green.
- The hardware unit docs 02–09 take their per-unit values from doc 21 and
  `k3_mc_baseline.json#computeDieCandidate`.
